Season 1 Recap & Evolution

  • PXJ Token & Liquidity: Launched on WAX with liquidity pools spanning Alcor, TacoSwap, and R-WAX ($WPIXAL).
  • Staking & Monthly Snapshots: Over 30% of Season 1 PXJ allocation fuels staking rewards alongside end-of-month snapshot distributions.
  • PFP Collection & Recycling: 10,000 Pixals minted; 1,550+ burned into Ingredient Packs for advanced crafting blends.
  • Multi-Chain Footprint: Expanded across WAX, Ethereum, Polygon, Base, and BNB Chain.

Season 2 Preparation & Modular Portals

  • Modular PX Portals: Staking, Market, Unpacker, Swap, and Learn portals built to connect across chains via third-party contracts.
  • Animal-Themed PX Characters: Preparing bears, ducks, cows/bulls, cats, dogs, beavers, bees, sheep, and more with mini-games and crossover perks.
  • Wax Pebbles & PixelPads: Wax Pebbles offer a crafting path toward future Wax Rocks, while Wax Rocks on PixelPads function as both Rock and Pixal PFP.

The WaxRocks CouncilThe WaxRocks Council

Foundations of the PixelDAO / Staking & Market Fee Governance

The WaxRocks Council represents the highest foundational tier of our community-governance mechanics, growing into a full PixelDAO as we progress along our shared Pixel Journey. Holders of WaxRocks and WaxRocks on PixelPads (which function as both Rock and Pixal PFP, alongside upcoming Wax Pebbles crafting paths) gain access to council lounges in our Discord, a 10% Discord XP boost, and monthly governance votes directing a 25% share of creator market fee earnings:

01 // Community Airdrops WAX and PXJ airdrop rewards for the active Discord community and special trait holders.
02 // Liquidity & LP Boosts Added project liquidity and TacoSwap / Alcor LP reward boosts in WAX, PXJ, TACO, or TLM.
03 // NFT Reward Pools Injecting curated Pixel NFTs and partner project collectibles into staking reward pools.
04 // Promotional Quests Funding community contests, educational guides, streamers, and outreach campaigns.
05 // Builder Recognition Rewarding special community-acknowledged contributions and council initiatives.
06 // Ecosystem Alliances Steering which partner projects across WAX and EVM we collaborate with next.
